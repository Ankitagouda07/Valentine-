Our Nights

Falling asleep with the call on became our tradition. You talking half sleepy, me pretending not to be tired. The phone between us feels like a thin pillow. Even silence with you is never awkward. Sometimes the call drops and I feel empty. I imagine the day we won’t need screens anymore. No network problems, just us in one room. Until then these calls are my favorite place. They remind me distance is temporary. But what we have is not.
